Where West Virginia stands

In West Virginia, 0 of 13 trades have a floor above the middle state's for that trade and 13 have a lower one. The highest floor is junk removal (2-person crew) at $66 an hour and the lowest is pet sitting and dog walking at $21; 1 of 13 trades are $40 or more. One place higher is Arkansas ($23); one place lower is Louisiana ($22).

How the house cleaning floor works out in West Virginia

The BLS median wage for maids and housekeeping cleaners in West Virginia is $13.51. Adding the employer's 7.65% payroll tax share, which a self-employed person pays too, gives $14.54. Only 75% of working hours can be billed, so dividing by 0.75 gives $19.39. Adding about $4.00 an hour of business costs (a typical $400 a month over 100 billable hours) gives $23.39, which rounds to the $23 floor. The recommended price of $29 adds a 20% profit margin.
