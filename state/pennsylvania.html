Where Pennsylvania stands

In Pennsylvania, 5 of 13 trades have a floor above the middle state's for that trade and 2 have a lower one. The highest floor is junk removal (2-person crew) at $74 an hour and the lowest is pet sitting and dog walking at $23; 2 of 13 trades are $40 or more. One place higher is Missouri ($27); one place lower is Virginia ($27).

How the house cleaning floor works out in Pennsylvania

The BLS median wage for maids and housekeeping cleaners in Pennsylvania is $16.29. Adding the employer's 7.65% payroll tax share, which a self-employed person pays too, gives $17.54. Only 75% of working hours can be billed, so dividing by 0.75 gives $23.38. Adding about $4.00 an hour of business costs (a typical $400 a month over 100 billable hours) gives $27.38, which rounds to the $27 floor. The recommended price of $34 adds a 20% profit margin.
