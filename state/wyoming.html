Where Wyoming stands

In Wyoming, 0 of 13 trades have a floor above the middle state's for that trade and 8 have a lower one. The highest floor is junk removal (2-person crew) at $68 an hour and the lowest is pet sitting and dog walking at $22; 1 of 13 trades are $40 or more. One place higher is Ohio ($26); one place lower is Georgia ($25).

How the house cleaning floor works out in Wyoming

The BLS median wage for maids and housekeeping cleaners in Wyoming is $15.32. Adding the employer's 7.65% payroll tax share, which a self-employed person pays too, gives $16.49. Only 75% of working hours can be billed, so dividing by 0.75 gives $21.99. Adding about $4.00 an hour of business costs (a typical $400 a month over 100 billable hours) gives $25.99, which rounds to the $26 floor. The recommended price of $32 adds a 20% profit margin.
