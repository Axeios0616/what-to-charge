Key findings

  • Across 13 trades, a self-employed worker has to charge roughly 1.5 to 2.1 times the employee wage per hour just to match an employee's pay after costs.
  • A solo house cleaner needs at least $21 an hour in Mississippi and $40 in Hawaii. In 15 of 51 states (incl. DC) the floor is $30 or more.
  • House cleaning: $21 an hour in Mississippi to $40 in Hawaii; middle state $28.
  • Window cleaning: $24 an hour in Louisiana to $35 in Washington; middle state $29.
  • Carpet cleaning: $31 an hour in Louisiana to $43 in Washington; middle state $36.
  • Gutter cleaning: $27 an hour in Louisiana to $39 in Washington; middle state $32.
  • Pressure washing: $28 an hour in Louisiana to $38 in District of Columbia; middle state $33.
  • Car detailing: $23 an hour in Louisiana to $33 in District of Columbia; middle state $28.
  • Lawn care: $27 an hour in Mississippi to $39 in Massachusetts; middle state $32.
  • Snow removal: $30 an hour in Mississippi to $42 in Massachusetts; middle state $35.
  • Christmas light installation: $28 an hour in Mississippi to $40 in Massachusetts; middle state $33.
  • House painting: $34 an hour in Arkansas to $54 in Hawaii; middle state $41.
  • Handyman: $32 an hour in West Virginia to $47 in District of Columbia; middle state $39.
  • Pet sitting and dog walking: $20 an hour in Mississippi to $32 in District of Columbia; middle state $25.
  • Junk removal (2-person crew): $64 an hour in Louisiana to $79 in Washington; middle state $71.

Method

Wages: state median hourly wage from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025, for the closest occupation: maids and housekeeping cleaners (house cleaning); janitors and cleaners (window, carpet and gutter cleaning); cleaners of vehicles and equipment (pressure washing, car detailing); landscaping and groundskeeping workers (lawn care, snow removal, Christmas lights); painters, construction and maintenance; maintenance and repair workers, general (handyman); animal caretakers (pet sitting); hand laborers and material movers (junk removal, counted twice for a two-person crew).

Floor per billable hour = median wage × 1.0765 (the employer's 7.65% share of payroll tax that a self-employed person also pays) ÷ 0.75 (share of working hours that are billable) + monthly business costs ÷ billable hours a month. Costs and hours are each trade's typical defaults in TidyRate's calculators (for example $400 a month and 100 billable hours for house cleaning). Recommended = floor ÷ (1 − profit margin), with margins of 15% to 25% by trade.

Limits: medians describe employees, not business owners; costs vary widely; floors exclude health insurance, retirement and paid time off, so they are a minimum, not a target.
