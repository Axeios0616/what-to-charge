Why charging the employee wage loses money

An employee is paid for every hour at work. An owner is only paid for hours a client is billed, and pays for the van, supplies, insurance, phone and the employer's share of payroll tax out of the same price. These floors assume 75% of working hours are billable and use each trade's typical monthly costs. Full method.
