Pet sitting and dog walking prices by state (2026): the least to charge per hour

A one-person pet sitting and dog walking business has to charge at least $20 an hour in Mississippi and $32 in District of Columbia just to earn what that state pays an employee for the same kind of work, after business costs. The middle state's floor is $25, about 1.5 times the employee wage.

Minimum and recommended hourly price, by state

Floor = break-even at the state's median wage for animal caretakers. Recommended adds a 15% profit margin. Per billable hour, so drive time and quoting are already covered.
