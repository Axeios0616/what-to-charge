House painting prices by state (2026): the least to charge per hour

A one-person house painting business has to charge at least $34 an hour in Arkansas and $54 in Hawaii just to earn what that state pays an employee for the same kind of work, after business costs. The middle state's floor is $41, about 1.7 times the employee wage.

Minimum and recommended hourly price, by state

Floor = break-even at the state's median wage for painters, construction and maintenance. Recommended adds a 25% profit margin. Per billable hour, so drive time and quoting are already covered.
