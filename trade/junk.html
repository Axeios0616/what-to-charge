Junk removal prices by state (2026): the least to charge per hour

A one-person junk removal business has to charge at least $64 an hour in Louisiana and $79 in Washington just to earn what that state pays an employee for the same kind of work, after business costs. The middle state's floor is $71, about 1.8 times the employee wage.

Minimum and recommended hourly price, by state

Floor = break-even at the state's median wage for laborers and freight, stock, and material movers, hand (two workers). Recommended adds a 20% profit margin. Per billable hour, so drive time and quoting are already covered.
