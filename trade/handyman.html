Handyman prices by state (2026): the least to charge per hour

A one-person handyman business has to charge at least $32 an hour in West Virginia and $47 in District of Columbia just to earn what that state pays an employee for the same kind of work, after business costs. The middle state's floor is $39, about 1.6 times the employee wage.

Minimum and recommended hourly price, by state

Floor = break-even at the state's median wage for maintenance and repair workers, general. Recommended adds a 20% profit margin. Per billable hour, so drive time and quoting are already covered.
