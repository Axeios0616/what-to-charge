Pressure washing prices by state (2026): the least to charge per hour

A one-person pressure washing business has to charge at least $28 an hour in Louisiana and $38 in District of Columbia just to earn what that state pays an employee for the same kind of work, after business costs. The middle state's floor is $33, about 2.0 times the employee wage.

Minimum and recommended hourly price, by state

Floor = break-even at the state's median wage for cleaners of vehicles and equipment. Recommended adds a 25% profit margin. Per billable hour, so drive time and quoting are already covered.
