Gutter cleaning prices by state (2026): the least to charge per hour

Published October 26, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

A one-person gutter cleaning business has to charge at least $27 an hour in Louisiana and $39 in Washington just to earn what that state pays an employee for the same kind of work, after business costs. The middle state's floor is $32, about 1.9 times the employee wage.

Minimum and recommended hourly price, by state

Floor = break-even at the state's median wage for janitors and cleaners, except maids and housekeeping cleaners. Recommended adds a 20% profit margin. Per billable hour, so drive time and quoting are already covered.

How to read the gutter cleaning numbers

In 44 of 51 states (including DC) the gutter cleaning floor is $30 an hour or more. The five highest are Massachusetts $39, Washington $39, California $36, New York $36, Vermont $36. The five lowest are Mississippi $27, Louisiana $27, Arkansas $28, West Virginia $29, South Carolina $29. The recommended price adds a profit margin of 20%, a median of $8 an hour above the floor.

In Delaware, the middle state, the BLS median wage is $16.98. The same steps as every trade (payroll tax share, then dividing by the 75% of hours that are billed, then the trade's typical business costs) give a floor of $32.

Wages come from the BLS occupation Janitors and Cleaners, Except Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners (37-2011), May 2025. Medians describe employees, so treat the floor as a minimum, not a target.
