Lawn care prices by state (2026): the least to charge per hour

A one-person lawn care business has to charge at least $27 an hour in Mississippi and $39 in Massachusetts just to earn what that state pays an employee for the same kind of work, after business costs. The middle state's floor is $32, about 1.7 times the employee wage.

Minimum and recommended hourly price, by state

Floor = break-even at the state's median wage for landscaping and groundskeeping workers. Recommended adds a 20% profit margin. Per billable hour, so drive time and quoting are already covered.
