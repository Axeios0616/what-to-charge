Car detailing prices by state (2026): the least to charge per hour

Published October 26, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

A one-person car detailing business has to charge at least $23 an hour in Louisiana and $33 in District of Columbia just to earn what that state pays an employee for the same kind of work, after business costs. The middle state's floor is $28, about 1.7 times the employee wage.

How to read the car detailing numbers

In 19 of 51 states (including DC) the car detailing floor is $30 an hour or more. The five highest are District of Columbia $33, Alaska $32, Washington $32, Colorado $31, Massachusetts $31. The five lowest are Louisiana $23, West Virginia $24, Texas $24, Mississippi $24, Alabama $24. The recommended price adds a profit margin of 20%, a median of $7 an hour above the floor.

In Arizona, the middle state, the BLS median wage is $17.02. The same steps as every trade (payroll tax share, then dividing by the 75% of hours that are billed, then the trade's typical business costs) give a floor of $28.

Wages come from the BLS occupation Cleaners of Vehicles and Equipment (53-7061), May 2025. Medians describe employees, so treat the floor as a minimum, not a target.
