Who it is for

Coaches, podcasters, bloggers, newsletter writers, group admins and creators whose audience runs a home-service business, rents out a home, or wants to budget better. It is not for coupon or deal sites, and not for anyone who cannot say it is an affiliate link.

Say it is an affiliate link

The US Federal Trade Commission requires you to disclose a commission clearly, close to the link and before it, not buried at the end or behind another link (FTC Endorsement Guides: what people are asking). Wording that works:

  • In a post, email or blog article: "I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post."
  • On a pin or in a video: start the description with "Paid link" or the sentence above, and say it out loud near the start of a video too.

"Affiliate link" on its own is not always clear to readers, so use one of the sentences above. We may look at posts that use your link.

Do I have to say it is an affiliate link?

Yes. See the disclosure wording below. It is the law in the US and it keeps your audience's trust.
